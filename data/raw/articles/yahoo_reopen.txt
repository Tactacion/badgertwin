AC returns to dozens of UW-Madison buildings weeks after pipe break
Kelly Meyerhofer, Milwaukee Journal Sentinel

The University of Wisconsin-Madison reopened dozens of buildings Aug. 10 that it closed earlier this summer due to a water main break.
A chilled water line broke June 17 , a utility failure that affected nearly every UW-Madison building, UW officials said at a UW Board of Regents meeting in July . To ensure available air conditioning was directed to the most important priorities, such as critical research and patient care, AC was shut down in some buildings and led to higher-than-normal temperatures in others.
Altogether, 34 buildings of the university's more than 400 were affected, including Bascom Hall.

UW-Madison remained open despite the break . Officials relocated some summer classes, and asked employees in affected buildings to work from home or relocate.
Repairs took nearly two months because the broken pipe was 4 feet in diameter and 20 feet underground.
